# C4: The creator merch market

*Background research, sources checked 2026-10-11. Published marketing statistics are reports or
estimates, not independently verified performance. **UNSOURCED** means no public evidence was
found.*

## Findings

- **Arcade already has creator storefronts.** Its terms (§10) say approved sellers keep "all markup
  above Arcade's generated price, plus 5% of that generated price". Approved "Entrepreneurs"
  (at least 25,000 TikTok or Instagram followers, or media outlets) get markup plus 7.5%. Payment
  comes within 30 days, through Trolley. [Terms §10](https://legal.arcade.ai/terms). Public
  examples: [a creator gallery](https://www.arcade.ai/hi/KarlieKloss) and
  [a maker storefront](https://www.arcade.ai/storefront/Stellar?tab=in-real-life). Arcade's
  [November 2025 launch](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/design-it-make-it-gift-it-arcade-launches-expansion-into-home-decor-luxury-brand-partnerships-and-first-ai-product-design-agent--all-in-time-for-holiday-gifting-302619154.html)
  named curated storefronts. So the opening for a new product is choosing the pieces and saying
  why, not "Arcade has no storefronts".
- **The money is the main risk.** At the public 5% tier, a $100 generated price earns the seller
  $5. Selling it at $120 earns $25, but only if markup is allowed and buyers pay it. Most creators
  with 1k–200k followers sit at the 5% tier.
- **Sizing anchors** (each from a different platform, none is Arcade's own): Fourthwall reports
  about $58 per order, with tees at 54.6% of merch sales and posters/wall art at 2.3%. Fanjoy's
  founder (2021) cited 2–4% conversion, with the denominator unclear. Arcade's own order value and
  conversion are UNSOURCED.
- **Size demand with a funnel, not a follower multiplier.** Orders = followers × launch reach ×
  store-visit rate × visitor conversion (the share of visitors who buy). For example, 1,000
  qualified visits × 2–4% = 20–40 orders, or $1,160–$2,320 at $58. That is a conditional
  illustration, not a forecast.
- **How creators pick designs today:** audience polls, hired designers ($10–50 for one listing on
  Fourthwall's directory), templates, merch teams (about six weeks at Fanjoy), and launching to see
  what sells. None starts from the creator's own photos.
- **UNSOURCED:** "fans will buy jewelry, rugs and ceramics made from a creator's photos". Tees
  and hoodies dominate the category mix, which cuts against it. *The Handmade Effect* finds
  handmade goods feel more meaningful, most strongly as gifts to loved ones. That supports gifting
  more than fan merch.

## Common claims that stay UNSOURCED

- "0.5–2% of followers buy each drop" ([Custom Ink](https://www.customink.com/blog/how-to-make-creator-merch/)): no cohort or method behind it.
- "Creator merch order value $48.50, 42% margin, 78% POD", and "68% of drop sales happen in 12 hours" ([example](https://voxbooster.com/blog/merch-sales-creator-statistics-2026/)): the source links don't lead to the named data.
- "Handmade or premium beats print-on-demand for small creators."
- "Audience polls predict purchases", or "AI-generated designs improve conversion".

## What follows

Every forecast input needs a denominator, a time window and a population. Before using any of
these numbers for an Arcade storefront, ask Arcade whether a seller can mark up and what the
Entrepreneur rate is in practice. Then measure a pilot: completed-order profit per qualified
visitor, creator earnings and refunds.
