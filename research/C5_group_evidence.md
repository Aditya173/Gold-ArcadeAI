# C5: Market evidence for ideas 2–6

*Background research, sources checked 2026-10-11. US figures where available, with other
geographies labelled. "Market size" here means a count of possible buyers, not a dollar market
for Arcade. Listed prices are asking prices, not measured spending. Each verdict is an
**UNSOURCED inference**, not a buyer test.*

## Summary

| Idea | Strongest evidence | Biggest gap | Inferred fit |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 Cafés | 412,498 US independent restaurant locations (Technomic, end of 2025), down 2.3% in 2025. Russ & Daughters' $20 storefront mug and Heath's Tartine glaze are direct precedents | No average café merch revenue share (the "30%" is one 2016 café-gallery case) | Ceramics, prints and textiles of the space; not rugs or jewelry |
| 3 Couples | 2.13M US marriages (2024); 82% register; average guest gift $150; 75% include a cash fund (The Knot 2026) | Keepsake and anniversary spend unmeasured; the wedding photographer usually owns copyright | The strongest segment; a registry must sit beside cash funds |
| 4 Travel | €104 souvenir spend on the last trip (one French/Portuguese sample); photo books are a proven category (CEWE, 6.32M books in 2025) | No measured shift toward personalised souvenirs | Prints and pendants; maker objects need a price test |
| 5 Hosts | Shop-the-room precedents: Minoan (QR code in the property), Fogo Island Inn shop, Hospitality Design examples | No host merch budget; Minoan's metrics are labelled illustrative | A guest-facing channel; weak evidence that hosts pay |
| 6 Clubs | OrderMyGear Q2 2025: spirit-wear cart $81.98, 2.8 items, 21 orders per pop-up store | Demand for non-apparel group pieces unproven; university trademark rules | Pendants, pins and prints as an add-on to apparel |

Multiplying a population count by an average spend does **not** give a market size. None of these
counts are buyers of photo-derived products.

## 2. Independent cafés, restaurants, bakeries, food trucks

- **Size:** 412,498 US independent restaurant locations at the end of 2025
  ([Technomic, p. 3](https://pages.winsightmedia.com/rs/561-ZNP-897/images/US-Industry-Insights-Technomic-020926.pdf));
  ~65,000 independent coffee shops in one directory ([Joe](https://joe.coffee/locations/));
  12,487 mobile-food employer establishments in 2023
  ([Census](https://data.census.gov/profile/72233_-_Mobile_food_services?n=72233)).
- **Prices:** Russ & Daughters storefront mug $20
  ([order page](https://russdaughters.toast.site/order/russanddaughters/item-passover-desserts_72422e5e-8de8-40fc-8e30-869c90a99eef));
  Levain tote $15, tee $35, candle $46 ([merch](https://levainbakery.com/collections/merch)).
- **Revenue share:** one case study, from 2016: merchandise was 30% of a café-gallery's revenue
  ([Fresh Cup](https://freshcup.com/merchandising/)). There is no industry average (UNSOURCED).
- **Precedents:** the Russ & Daughters mug reproduces the storefront and the tile floor. Heath
  sells a $45 bowl in a glaze developed with Tartine
  ([Heath](https://www.heathceramics.com/products/dessert-bowl-2?variant=6967132389409)). Kogi
  trucks sold collaboration merch ([case](https://www.nickandkatiecreative.com/frxrc_stadiumdog)).
- **Risks:** the operator base is shrinking (−2.3% in 2025). UNSOURCED: customers may want the
  storefront and the signature dish, not photos of diners. Maker prices may exceed what owners can
  resell at.

## 3. Couples

- **Size:** 2,128,446 US marriages in 2024, provisional
  ([CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/data/dvs/marriage-divorce/national-marriage-divorce-rates-00-24.pdf)).
  82% of couples create a registry
  ([The Knot 2026](https://www.theknotww.com/press-releases/the-knot-worldwide-releases-2026-annual-registry-study),
  1,759 respondents).
- **Spend:** $150 average guest gift
  ([The Knot](https://www.theknot.com/content/how-much-to-spend-on-wedding-gifts)). 75% include a
  cash fund and 41% want cash most. In a different sample, 87% of couples on Zola add a cash fund
  ([Zola 2025](https://www.zola.com/expert-advice/the-first-look-report-2025)).
- **Prices:** Artifact Uprising wedding albums from $159, photo-wrapped hardcovers $40
  ([collection](https://www.artifactuprising.com/wedding)); a handwriting woven blanket $159
  ([Frankie Print Co](https://shop.app/m/a46kmwpvg2/collections/61230743610/woven-throw-blankets)).
- **Risks:** cash competes with physical gifts. The photographer generally owns the copyright in
  commissioned wedding photos ([US Copyright Office](https://www.copyright.gov/help/faq/faq-fairuse.html)).
  UNSOURCED: likeness and delivery timing may matter more than novelty, and proofs may reduce
  disappointment.

## 4. Travellers

- **Size:** 1.52 billion international arrivals in 2025, up 4% (these count trips, not people)
  ([UN Tourism](https://pre-webunwto.s3.eu-west-1.amazonaws.com/s3fs-public/2026-01/260120-international-tourist-arrivals-up-4-in-2025-reflecting-strong-travel-demand-around-the-world-en.pdf)).
- **Spend:** €104 on souvenirs and gifts on the last trip, in a French/Portuguese nature-tourism
  sample ([World Bank, p. 58](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099154307032432625/pdf/IDU165055bbe1a6ac142b019b601211ae894e100.pdf));
  £51 on UK one-day overseas trips ([Confused.com](https://www.confused.com/press/releases/2026/extreme-day-trips-rise-popularity-set-brits-back-579-on-average)).
- **Photo products:** Artifact Uprising travel books $19–59
  ([books](https://www.artifactuprising.com/photo-books?broad_book_type=Layflat)). CEWE sold 6.32M
  photo books in 2025, up 3.5% ([results](https://ir.cewe.de/nachrichten/cewe-umsatz-und-ergebnis-2025-weiter-gesteigert-alle-ziele-erreicht/5cee191d-29ad-4e41-9951-99037850934b)).
  In a German survey, 56.2% named holidays among their top photo subjects
  ([CEWE/Civey](https://www.cewe-group.com/en/press/cewe-germany/press-releases/detail.html?cHash=762f90c12f837c6f00aa278223e51c99&tx_news_pi1%5Baction%5D=detail&tx_news_pi1%5Bcontroller%5D=News&tx_news_pi1%5Bnews%5D=1661)).
- **Risks (UNSOURCED):** an order after the trip loses the urgency of buying a souvenir on site.
  Bulky goods add shipping friction. Demand for photo books may not carry over to jewelry or rugs.

## 5. Short-term-rental hosts and boutique hotels

- **Size:** 5.5M+ Airbnb hosts and 9M+ active listings ([Airbnb](https://news.airbnb.com/about-us));
  2,313 US boutique properties in 2025
  ([CoStar](https://www.costar.com/article/1969001935/what-the-2025-numbers-are-really-telling-us-about-boutique-hotels?edition=us)).
- **Spend:** host merch budgets and guest retail spend per stay are UNSOURCED. Hoxton lists
  room-care products at £15 each ([HoxShop](https://shop.thehoxton.com/collections/all)).
- **Precedents:** Minoan's free showroom, where guests find it through a QR code and the host earns
  a commission ([Minoan](https://minoan.com/how-it-works/showroom)). Guests buying furnishings in
  hotels ([Hospitality Design](https://hospitalitydesign.com/projects/shoppable-hotels-trend/157205/)).
  Fogo Island Inn's shop ([shop](https://fogoislandworkshops.ca/collections/fogo-island-inn-shop)).
  Airbnb × Etsy "Art of Hosting" ([announcement](https://news.airbnb.com/en-uk/airbnb-and-etsy-invite-you-to-explore-the-art-of-hosting)).
- **Risks:** Minoan labels its example metrics illustrative. UNSOURCED: guests may buy what felt
  good to use, not a photo-derived version, and hosts may prefer commission over buying inventory.

## 6. Clubs, teams and student organisations

- **Size:** 1M Strava clubs in 2025, with running clubs up 3.5×
  ([Strava](https://press.strava.com/en-gb/articles/strava-releases-12th-annual-year-in-sport-trend-report-2025));
  900+ North American climbing gyms ([CBJ](https://climbingbusinessjournal.com/2025-cbj-gym-list-awards/));
  500+ student organisations at one university ([Harvard](https://dso.college.harvard.edu/student-organization-fair)).
- **Order benchmarks:** spirit wear $81.98 per cart (2.8 items), fundraisers $60.52, uniforms and
  equipment $156.16, 21 orders per pop-up store
  ([OrderMyGear Q2 2025](https://www.ordermygear.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Q2-Online-Store-Benchmark-Report.pdf)).
- **Precedents:** Custom Ink club apparel with per-member payment
  ([clubs](https://www.customink.com/audience/community/clubs-organizations)); running-shop
  community tees at $33–43 ([BRC](https://www.brooklynrunningco.com/shop/originals/)).
- **Risks:** universities require licensed vendors for trademarked goods
  ([Harvard rules](https://trademark.harvard.edu/guidelines-ordering-internal-insignia-items-swag)).
  UNSOURCED: demand for required apparel may not carry over to optional decorative goods.

## What follows

Treat population → existing spending → demand for this product as three separate claims. A
wishlist, an asking price or a uniform order is not keepsake revenue. Test the buyer, the occasion
and the product together.
