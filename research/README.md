# Research

Background research done before the hackathon, from public sources checked on 2026-10-11. Each
note keeps its source links. **UNSOURCED** marks a claim or recommendation that no public
evidence was found for. None of these notes describes how the Gold SDK reads photos.

| Note | Question | Headline |
|---|---|---|
| [C1](C1_taste_models.md) | Can a model read *taste* from photos? | Generic aesthetic scores predict the crowd and mostly re-read quality. Personal taste is a small offset on top, and it is fragile on unseen people. Photographing something is not evidence of liking it |
| [C2](C2_vlm_grounded_reading.md) | Can a small open VLM describe photos without inventing things? | Benchmarks only screen for object errors. One photo per request, a ledger of photo-ID evidence and a verification pass matter more than the model |
| [C3](C3_brand_extract_tools.md) | What tools already turn images into a brand or style? | None documents "many personal photos → one identity". Adobe Express and Recraft come closest. A catalogue of failure modes to design against |
| [C4](C4_creator_merch.md) | Is there a creator merch market on Arcade? | Arcade already has storefronts. A seller earns markup + 5% of the generated price (7.5% for approved Entrepreneurs). Tees dominate merch; price is the risk |
| [C5](C5_group_evidence.md) | Is there evidence for each group in ideas 2–6? | Couples are the strongest segment; cafés, travellers and clubs are plausible at low prices; hosts look like a channel more than a buyer |
| [C6](C6_photo_consent.md) | What do consent and privacy require? | Start with the system picker; keep reading, keeping and publishing as three separate choices; face recognition carries the legal risk |
