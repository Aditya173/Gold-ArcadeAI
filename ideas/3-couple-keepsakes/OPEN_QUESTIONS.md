# Idea 3: open questions

1. **Does a form give the same input?** A buyer can type a date, a city and two colours in twenty seconds,
   and Etsy already sells coordinate necklaces that way. Nobody has shown that pieces from two rolls are
   picked over pieces from that form, blind. Until then the pendant is, honestly, a form product.
2. **No real couple yet.** Two consenting people who travelled together need to run the two-roll reading
   and check the shared moments it finds against what actually happened.
3. **No buyer asked yet.** "Would you pay ~$250 for this pair?" has not been asked of anyone.
4. **Guests vs cash.** Most couples ask for cash; no guest has been asked to chip in toward a made object.
   Nothing more is built on the registry until that test runs.
5. **Engraving at pendant scale.** Fine text and coordinates are classic maker refusals. The brief keeps to
   a line and a dot; no maker has read it.
6. **Is the chain included?** The mock prices one pendant unit; a real `products/get` has to say.
7. **Couple approval.** Per-piece and per-line approval is designed, not built, so the guest page is a
   preview only.
8. **Category ids.** Rug, print and pillow ids are inferred until `categories/list` runs on the day.
