# Idea 2: open questions

## Main line (café owner)

1. **No café has been read.** Everything here runs on an invented owner. One real owner shoot is the first
   step.
2. **Does a form give the same input?** "What do you serve?" and "what colours is your room?" take twenty
   seconds. The room set from photos has to beat the form line in the owner's own blind pick, with the
   form filled first.
3. **Does the room stand out at all?** A café's materials may be ordinary against other people's food
   photos. If the SDK reports nothing distinctive, the whys must say "a design starting point", not a
   finding.
4. **No buyer asked yet.** 3–5 owners need to see the four pieces at their prices and say yes or no, piece
   by piece, and separately for the rug.
5. **Printed vs tufted rug.** Arcade's listed $576 rug is wool. A printed rug has no Arcade quote; the
   nearest public figure is a print-on-demand base cost. No set total until it is priced.
6. **Do ceramics and table textiles exist on Arcade?** Unconfirmed until `categories/list`. A plate stays
   decorative until a maker confirms a food-safe glaze.
7. **Logos.** Owners will ask for theirs; Arcade's rules on logos and text are unknown.

## Fork 1 (the diner)

1. **Is it worth more than a $15 towel?** Custom tea towels sell for about $15–23; the diner line has to
   justify an artisan price.
2. **Loom-woven textiles aren't printable.** The tea towel brief must be a printed towel; a woven one is a
   different product a print maker would decline.
3. **Does a form give the same input?** "Your three favourite dishes" is a form too.
4. **No buyer asked yet:** a "would buy or gift at this price" check against a $15 towel.
5. **Where someone eats is sensitive.** No place names anywhere; the gift page is count- and place-free.
