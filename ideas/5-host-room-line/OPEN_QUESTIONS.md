# Idea 5: open questions

The honest blockers from the reviews.

1. **No buyer asked yet.** No host has seen a board or a real quote. Show it to 2 or 3 multi-unit
   hosts with a real Arcade price for a 5x8 rug: would you order, for which units, when, at what
   price?
2. **Do real portfolios share a palette at all?** The demo host is invented, so its house colours are
   true by construction. Whether rooms from one real host share more colours than rooms from
   different hosts is untested. If not, the pitch is "one room photo in, one pillow out".
3. **Do the photos beat a 30-second form?** A host can type wall, floor, sofa and an accent per room.
   The photos only earn their place if they catch a recurring colour the host would leave out and
   it changes a piece. Untested on a real host.
4. **Made-to-order lead times vs a host's need for quick replacement.** Rental pieces get stained or
   damaged between guests. Weeks of lead time means keeping a spare per unit or living with a gap.
   Does the host need washable pieces?
5. **Is this just Magic Match, batched?** Arcade already turns one room photo into matched rugs. If it
   already handles several rooms, the remaining new part is the guest page.
6. **Pieces can vanish into the room.** A rug in the house colours may match the floor too well. The
   pipeline does not yet check that a piece stands out from the surfaces it sits on; it has no notion
   of which colour is the floor or the wall.
7. **Price vs stock.** A made-to-order room (rug, print, pillow) costs roughly twice a stock fit-out
   on mock estimates. Real Arcade prices, trade terms and the seller markup are unknown until the day.
8. **Guest sales are thin.** The best small-hotel precedent sells "a few pieces a week". The guest
   line is a side line, not the business.
9. **Thresholds are choices.** "Recurs in 2+ rooms", the colour-distance tolerance and the accent
   saturation cut are not measured.
