# Idea 4: open questions

The honest blockers from the reviews, before anyone claims this works.

1. **Photos may only find the place and month. Does a 3-question form do as well?** Finding the trip
   is something Apple and Google Photos already do for free. If a trip's colours and motifs, as the
   reading gives them, are no closer to "this trip" than what a traveller types in (what you did, the
   mood, colours you liked), the photos add nothing after the trip is found. Test: the blind
   photo-vs-form-vs-generic comparison in FRAMEWORK §8.
2. **Would a brief fit another trip just as well?** Pattern ideas built from kinds of place and a
   palette tend to read as "any city". The swap test (place names removed: which trip is this?) has
   to pass before a photo-built piece is offered.
3. **The one input a form cannot give is the photo itself.** A print painted from a photo the owner
   picks (opt-in `referenceIds`, print only) may be the real product. It is not built against the
   mock (the mock has no reference upload), and no one has been asked to buy it.
4. **No buyer asked yet.** Nobody has said "yes, at about $100". Per-trip souvenir spend sits around
   €100 for the whole trip, so a $100 piece is a gift or milestone purchase. Ask at least 5
   travellers other than the builder.
5. **Trips are unmeasured.** The SDK reports `trips` as unmeasured in the demo fixture, so every trip
   is a proposal until the owner confirms it. How often proposed trips are right (long stays, a
   metro area split into several towns, two short trips with a gap) is unknown.
6. **Thresholds are guesses.** 3 moments per capsule, the product sizes, and the retry wording are
   choices, not measurements.
7. **No human maker has read the briefs.** A tea-towel pattern or print at these sizes may need
   changes a maker would ask for.
8. **Arcade's live category names and prices** are unknown until `categories/list` on the day; the
   Textile and Wall Art Print entries are inferred.
