# Idea 4 research: a trip capsule for travellers

Market research and public sources only. Nothing here comes from reading anyone's photos. Every
number names its source. Where a figure is a vendor's or a blog's, the text says so.

## Verdict: keep, with the buyer narrowed

**The buyer is the traveller, after a trip, buying for themselves or as a gift for a travel
companion.** Travel *creators* selling a trip drop to their audience is really the creator
storefront (idea 1) with a trip filter, and no evidence was found that travel-creator audiences buy
trip-specific objects.

| Question | Answer | How strong |
|---|---|---|
| Who pays | The traveller, after the trip, for themselves or as a gift. A print or tea towel is the realistic first purchase; a pendant suits a milestone trip (a honeymoon, a first trip abroad) | Medium. People already pay for keepsakes made from their own trip (Polarsteps books, map prints, paintings of a holiday photo). No evidence yet that they pay maker-object prices |
| Why photos, not a form | Photos find the trip for you (place and month), and the image itself is the one input no form can supply. Whether the photos' colours and motifs beat what a traveller would type in a 3-question form is **open** (see `OPEN_QUESTIONS.md`) | Strong for finding trips; open for everything after that |
| Which photos | The traveller's own camera roll. The trip is proposed from place and date; the traveller confirms it and can drop photos | Strong: Apple and Google already teach users that "Trips" exist |
| Arcade categories | Wall Art Print, Textile (a tea towel), Pendant Necklace, Rug | Category names are a fixture until Arcade's live `categories/list` |

## 1. Market and willingness to pay

- **Travel volume.** About 1.52 billion international arrivals in 2025, up 4% (UN Tourism barometer;
  link not retained). In an arrivia Q2 2025 survey, 24% of Gen Z take 7+ trips a year, against 12%
  of all respondents; millennials and Gen X mostly take 3–6
  ([arrivia report](https://resources.arrivia.com/hubfs/Reports/arrivia%20-%20Report%20-%20Loyalty%20and%20the%20Changing%20Traveler%20Q2%202025.pdf?hsLang=en)).
  Gen Z and millennials are expected to make up half of US holiday travellers
  ([Deloitte](https://www.deloitte.com/us/en/insights/industry/retail-distribution/holiday-travel-survey.html?nc=42)).
- **Souvenir market.** Attraction-souvenir retail is estimated at about $26–29B in 2025 by paid
  research firms that disagree with each other, so it is directional only
  ([Business Research Co. via GII](https://www.giiresearch.com/report/tbrc2045228-attraction-souvenir-retail-global-market-report.html),
  [Market Intelo](https://marketintelo.com/report/attraction-souvenir-retail-market/amp),
  [Research and Markets](https://www.researchandmarkets.com/reports/6245015/attraction-souvenir-retail-market-report)).
  Technavio forecasts gifts, novelty and souvenirs together to grow $20.3B over 2024–29
  ([Technavio](https://technavio.com/report/gifts-novelty-and-souvenirs-market-industry-analysis)).
- **Spend per trip is low.** Published surveys put souvenir, gift and craft spend at roughly €100 for
  a whole trip (a European nature-tourism sample; link not retained). **A $100+ object is a gift or
  milestone purchase, not an impulse souvenir.**
- **Photos are already the souvenir.** In Opodo's 2025 poll of 9,000 travellers, 41% put making
  memories through photos or travel journals first, and 35% each buy magnets or local crafts and
  jewelry ([Globetrender](https://globetrender.com/2025/09/12/tattoos-join-trinkets-souvenirs-2025/),
  [Opodo](https://www.opodo.co.uk/blog/best-holiday-souvenirs/)).
- **Place matters to buyers.** A Purdue conjoint study (respondents rank combinations of features,
  which shows what they trade off) found American tourists prefer souvenirs tied to the local place
  or people ([Purdue thesis](https://hammer.purdue.edu/articles/thesis/Tourists_preferences_of_souvenir_design_based_on_expressive_attributes_a_cross-cultural_perspective/13084940)).
- **Don't cite:** a "65% of souvenir buyers want personalized" figure circulates
  ([ZipDo](https://zipdo.co/souvenir-industry-statistics/)) but names no original survey.

## 2. Who pays today, and for what

| Precedent | What it is | Price | What it shows |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Polarsteps** | Tracks a trip automatically and sells a hardback Travel Book made from it; the app is free | Not found in search | The closest precedent: **people pay for a keepsake built automatically from a trip.** 25M+ trips tracked ([25M trips](https://www.polarsteps.com/news/polarsteps-reaches-25-million-tracked-trips-and-celebrates-the-adventures-behind-the-number), [the book model](https://www.polarsteps.com/news/polarsteps-hits-1-million-users-and-raises-3-million-in-funding)) |
| Photo books (Artifact Uprising, CEWE) | Paper keepsakes from your photos | Roughly $19–$59 | Photo-to-object demand at scale, but paper |
| **Mapiful, Grafomap** | Custom map posters of a place you type in | About $49–$60, $104 framed (2017–18) ([BI](https://www.businessinsider.nl/grafomap-custom-map-poster-review-2017-9), [Mapiful PR](https://mynewsdesk.com/se/mapiful/pressreleases/mapiful-bring-your-favourite-places-home-1841864)) | A place made into a print sells. **It comes from a form: that is the baseline the photos have to beat** |
| Etsy coordinate and map jewelry | Engraved coordinates or a street-map pendant | About $12–$48 ([listing](https://www.etsy.com/listing/254229065), [shop](https://www.etsy.com/ca/shop/MapCraftShop)) | Travel jewelry exists at the cheap end, and it is form-based |
| Etsy push-pin travel maps | A map with pins, names and dates | Varies ([PushPinTravelMaps](https://www.etsy.com/ie/shop/PushPinTravelMaps)) | For frequent travellers, the *collection* of trips is itself a product |
| Etsy painting from a holiday photo | A designer paints one of your photos | $40+ to $115, $225 for large, 2–5 week turnaround ([listing](https://www.etsy.com/listing/4435014265), [$115 listing](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1195236532)) | People pay for one photo made into one piece |
| Pandora travel charms; Jet Set Candy | Mass-made destination charms | £48–£65 a charm ([Grace and Co](https://www.graceandcojewellery.co.uk/products/pandora-travel-dangle-charms), [Moodie Davitt](https://moodiedavittreport.com/pandora-to-introduce-first-inflight-exclusive-travel-charm), [Elite Traveler](https://elitetraveler.com/style/jet-set-candy-commemorates-travel-with-personal-jewelry)) | **People pay jewelry prices for a token of a trip**, but it is the same for everyone who went |
| Travel-creator merch | Fourthwall pitches "road trip merch" from $9.50 ([Fourthwall](https://fourthwall.com/make-your-own/road-trip-merch)) | Low print-on-demand prices | Travel creators earn from brand deals, presets and courses ([Sellfy](https://sellfy.com/blog/how-to-make-money-as-a-photographer/)). **No evidence found of trip-specific drops selling** |

**The gap:** keepsakes that are automatic and personal are paper (Polarsteps, photo books).
Keepsakes that are objects are generic (Pandora) or typed into a form (Mapiful, coordinate
pendants). A painting of your own photo is personal but takes designer time and weeks. No product
found turns one trip's photos into an object.

## 3. How travellers' photos exist and get shared

- **The camera roll already groups trips.** iOS 18 Photos has a "Trips" collection worked out from
  when you were away from home ([MacStories](https://www.macstories.net/stories/ios-and-ipados-18-the-macstories-review/9),
  [MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/guide/ios-18-photos/)); Google Photos has suggested trip
  albums since 2016 ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/?p=1295229),
  [TNW](https://thenextweb.com/news/google-photos-makes-albums-for-you)). **Finding trips is not a
  moat**; users already expect it. Apple's photo library API does not hand third-party apps the
  Trips collection, so the SDK proposes trips itself (`trips[]`).
- **Group trips go into shared albums**, where companions add their own photos
  ([Apple help](https://help.apple.com/photos/mac/1.0/en.lproj/pht7a4c765b.html),
  [Go Ahead Tours](https://www.goaheadtours.ca/travel-blog/articles/5-tips-for-sharing-photos-with-tour-group)).
  A shared album holds **other people's photos**, so consent is per contributor.
- **Saved images and screenshots** often carry an import date and sometimes someone else's
  location, so a trip should be built from the traveller's own camera photos.

## 4. Arcade fit (fixture names until the live category list)

| Piece | Category | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Trip print | Wall Art Print | `colors` from the trip palette (up to 10). Up to 3 `referenceIds` for an owner-picked photo, print only, opt-in |
| Trip tea towel | Textile | The cheapest piece; a good gift for a travel companion |
| Trip pendant | Pendant Necklace | No `colors` or `referenceIds` on jewelry; the prompt carries the motif. Highest maker-refusal risk |
| Years-of-trips rug | Rug | One band per trip palette, for frequent travellers. Hand-tufted rugs start around $400 ([The Hustle](https://thehustle.co/news/ai-made-that-rug-and-those-earrings)) |

Arcade blocks trademarked names and logos ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/?p=2905838)), so
prompts carry no landmark names or transit marks. Simple Arcade pieces cost about $100, above the
per-trip souvenir spend, so the print or tea towel is the first purchase.

## 5. What the Gold SDK adds

1. **Trips proposed from the roll** (`trips[]`: place, start, end, moments), from the owner's own
   photos, with the owner confirming each one.
2. **A reading of one trip's photos** (call `read()` on just that trip): the trip's signature and
   palette, so the print carries that trip's colours, not the person's usual ones.
3. **A "why" that quotes the reading exactly**, which lifts the piece above a typed-in poster.

Whether 2 and 3 tell a traveller anything a short form would not is the open question.

## 6. Risks

- **Weakest assumption:** that travellers pay maker-object prices for a trip. Precedents pay paper
  prices for personal keepsakes or jewelry prices for generic ones.
- **Commoditization:** Apple could add "print this trip". The defence is the object plus the
  evidence, not the trip grouping.
- **Other people's photos:** companions appear in shared albums; default to the owner's own photos.
- **Location is sensitive:** home is used only to tell trips from home and never leaves the device.
  Gift pages show the city and month only.
- **Invented reasons:** every "why" quotes the reading exactly (enforced by `planner.check`).

## 7. Alternates checked

- **Pet owners:** strong demand (pet portraits are an Etsy staple at $18–$225,
  [findniche](https://findniche.com/etsy-product/mini-custom-watercolor-pet-portrait-dog--1106950363)),
  but one good photo does the job, so a roll reading adds little.
- **Musicians, tattoo artists, real-estate agents:** their input is a curated upload or one listing.
- None fits better than trips. No swap.
