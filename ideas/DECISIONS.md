# Decisions: the open forks

Two ideas split into two materially different products during review. Both sides of each fork are
built, so the choice is between working code, not between plans. Neither side has been tested
with a buyer.

## Fork 1. Idea 1: whose audience is the reading for?

| | A: creator storefront (curated feed) | B: private camera-roll object |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | The creator's posted photos → palette pieces sold to fans; the creator earns Arcade's commission | The owner's own camera roll → pieces they buy for themselves or put on a gift list; nothing is published |
| For it | The feed is public already, so nothing private is published | The roll shows the life the feed leaves out, which is where a distinctive reading is likeliest |
| Against it | A curated feed is the creator's polished side, so it may say little a form couldn't. The money is thin at 5% | The reviewer found that the pieces looked alike whether the input was the reading or something else: the design rules, not the photos, were shaping them |
| Blocked on | Arcade's answers on markup and the Entrepreneur rate | The blind photos-vs-form test, judged by the owner |

**How to choose.**
- Ask Arcade the two money questions ([HACKATHON_DAY.md](../HACKATHON_DAY.md)). If a seller can
  mark up, A has a business; if not, A is a brand capsule rather than an income.
- Run the blind test on B: does the owner pick the photo version over the form version? If yes,
  B is the stronger demo, because "made from your roll" is a claim A cannot make.
- If A's money works *and* B passes its test, build B first and offer A as its public mode: the
  same reading, shown to fans.

## Fork 2. Idea 2: whose photos carry the food signal?

| | A: café owner line | B: diner line |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | The owner photographs their room → rug, print, pillow and plate for the café | A person's own food photos → tea towel, print, pillow and plate, for themselves or as a gift |
| For it | The motifs come from the room (materials, palette), which a menu form can't give | It can be tested on anyone's food photos today |
| Against it | No real café has been read yet. A room may not stand out against other people's food photos | A gift or self-purchase with no price test yet |
| Blocked on | One real owner: a shoot of the space, the owner grading a blind sheet, 3–5 owners asked to price | A willingness-to-pay check |

**How to choose.**
- If a café owner can shoot their space this week, run A: one shoot, a blind grading of the photo
  version against the form version, and a pricing question to a few owners.
- If not, run B on your own food photos now, and pair it with a simple price test (would you buy
  this at this price?).
- Both can run. B is cheaper and faster; A is the larger business if owners resell.
