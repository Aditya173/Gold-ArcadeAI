# Idea 1, fork 1 notes: the private camera-roll object

No new web research; market figures are in `../RESEARCH.md`.

- **Why the roll and not the feed.** A feed is the chosen public look, often one preset; a roll is the life
  behind it. If the reading carries anything a form can't, it is more likely to be in the roll. That is a
  hypothesis for the SDK's reliability numbers and the blind test to settle, not a finding.
- **Gifting is the better-supported occasion.** Research on handmade goods ("the handmade effect": handmade
  feels more meaningful, strongest for gifts to loved ones) supports a gift list more than fan merch.
- **Taste vs what you photograph.** Keep "I photographed this", "I like this", "I own this" and "I want
  this" apart. The whys say what the photos show, never what the owner likes.
- **Privacy, three decisions.** Which photos are read (the whole roll, on the device); which readings are
  kept (the owner's private page); what is published (nothing by default; the gift page carries no photo,
  why, idea or count).
- **Unknown:** how many people buy a $100–800 piece for themselves from their own roll, and Arcade's
  referral terms for someone who sends a buyer to checkout without being a seller.
