# Idea 1: open questions

Honest blockers. None is answered; each needs a person, Arcade, or the real SDK.

## Main line (creator storefront)

1. **The creator earns ~5% of a sale at Arcade's public commission.** On every mock-priced capsule, the
   creator's take on the median piece is below what one $45 print-on-demand hoodie leaves ($12.56). We
   need Arcade's answer on the entrepreneur-program rate and on whether a seller may mark up. If both are
   no, drop either the Gold fee or the "earn from your capsule" pitch.
2. **Does a form give the same input?** A creator can type a bio and pick five colours. Nobody has shown
   that a capsule built from the feed reading is picked as "more me" over one built from that form, blind.
3. **Is a curated feed readable at all?** A feed is small and edited with one preset. The SDK has to
   report a reliability for the signature on a 30–100 photo set; until then curated mode uses palette and
   place types only, and the palette is "the feed's colours".
4. **No buyer asked yet.** No creator has been asked to pay for a capsule, and no audience has been shown
   one at these prices.
5. **Printed vs tufted rug.** The mock prices the rug from a hand-tufted figure. A printed rug is a
   different product with no Arcade quote. Every rug brief says "printed".
6. **Category ids.** Only Rings and Pendant Necklace are in Arcade's docs; the others are inferred until
   `categories/list` runs on the day.
7. **Jewelry prices.** 14k pieces swing widely with metal and stones; real quotes may sit well above the
   mock.

## Fork 1 (the owner's camera roll)

1. **Does a form give the same input?** Same test, judged by the owner: is the set "more me" than what
   they would have typed?
2. **Would anyone buy a $100–800 piece for themself from their own roll?** No self-purchase figure exists.
3. **What does Gold earn?** No commission applies; a referral cut or a small fee needs Arcade's terms.
4. **Does the reading drive the design, or do the prompt rules?** If capsules from different readings all
   look alike, the photos are decoration. A swapped-reading arm is the check.
